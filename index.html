Inventory final resolution order
20–25 days → 7–10 days
🔴 Problem: 3 separate approval routes → confusion, missing deadlines, sequential approvals where parallel was possible.
🟢 Solution: Merged into 1 universal workflow; parallel steps, mandatory attachment, Accept/Reject mechanism.
📈 Result: 60% faster, no lost documents.
🛡️
Audit discrepancy (shortage/theft)
24 days → 14 days
🔴 Problem: Different workflows for shortage vs theft, no intermediate control points.
🟢 Solution: Unified workflows with "Loss type" classifier; intermediate control at day 7 for theft.
📈 Result: 40% faster resolution, full audit trail.
💸
Voluntary damage compensation
No "zombie" tasks
🔴 Problem: Tasks with no deadlines, employees could leave file uploads pending indefinitely.
🟢 Solution: Strict deadlines, enforced file upload before task completion, redefined task wording.
📈 Result: Zero pending tasks, full accountability.

Purchase requisition (PR)
Predictable 7–10 days
